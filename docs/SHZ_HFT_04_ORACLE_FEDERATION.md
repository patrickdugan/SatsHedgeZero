# SHZ-HFT-04: federated low-latency oracle and independent router economy

## Purpose

Use the TradeLayer model of independently operated private books and locally allocated collateral. A router controls its own matching queue and pricing service; no SHZ-wide sequencer, global matching mutex, or shared exchange balance. Each signed intent specifies one router and allocation generation. A smart client can solicit signed quotes from several routers, compare all-in cost and split orders into distinct, budgeted child intents; the user is free to operate their own router. Local price-time priority only; no global best-price or consolidated-liquidity guarantee. Keep route choice and market risk separate from oracle signing. SHZ's operator may earn ordinary router and feed service revenue by operating infrastructure under the same published rules as others.

## Two timing paths

Use the existing INDEX-03 Pyth Pro/Chainlink source authentication as a starting point; live API credentials, redistribution rights and end-to-end provider verification are distinct gates. TradeLayer wash-filtered VWAP is a slow independent reference for audits/anchors, not a mandatory subsecond feed. At a fixed 200 ms slot boundary T_k, assemble the newest eligible provider reports, normalize with deterministic age/skew/spread policy, agree one VALID midpoint or explicit HOLD packet. Reference prototype carries network/market/epoch/policy hash, sequence, previous packet hash, provider report digests/timestamps/prices or HOLD reason. Canonical full source reports, signer-rotation registry, availability proofs and native verifier must be retained externally. Do not accept unsigned fallback. Freshness max 1 second, source skew <=500 ms, spread <=50 bps in the reference; calibrate before any deployment.

The paid service releases the certified packet at scheduled T_k, public clear stream at T_k+200 ms. Both use the same packet body and quorum certificate. Late quorum formation or slow network reduces the realized advantage; it does not extend the public release deadline. The signed policy fixes cadence and schedule, not reception time. At 200 ms cadence the public view lags by one packet. Signed packet receipt timestamps, clock error and measured p50/p99 delivery at independent sites establish an SLA, not the Bitcoin block time. Public dissemination must have independently operated mirrors, bounded delivery, audit archives; charge only under verified service terms. The early release is explicit service differentiation and can be copied/leaked; no guaranteed exclusive information or market profit. A commitment to a salted packet may be announced before release for audit; availability and publication timestamps need independent observers. No price hiding on-chain beyond normal asynchronous checkpoint cadence. Bitcoin confirmation time is stochastic. Never advertise a 200 ms advantage over Bitcoin finality.

For V1, use 4 of 5 independently verifiable BIP340 signatures to certify one canonical digest per slot. Quorum intersection >=3, more than f=1 assumed Byzantine signer, provided honest signers have durable one-vote locks and fenced identity. 3-of-5 alone is not sufficient for this safety property: intersection may be only the Byzantine signer. Safety is conditional, availability requires four reachable honest/cooperative signers and a timely valid proposal. Existing deterministic FROST maturity fixture is not a production packet DKG; later use audited distributed key generation, separate packet signing keys/nonces, robust proposal/round protocol and signer attribution. Keep maturity attestation keys and nonce lifecycle distinct. The proof is not a guarantee of data truth or availability; a correlated upstream outage triggers HOLD. Do not reuse DLC event nonces for high-rate packets. Initial implementation uses four individual signatures, synthetic sources, in-memory locks, no real transport. A receiver accepts sequential certificates in its tier's bounded clock window and checks source age again at use. Missing sequence, HOLD, stale or future source, equivocation or clock uncertainty fails closed; catch-up/checkpoint and restart need a durable protocol.

## Order and risk policy

Premium intent authorization must bind feed/latency tier, tariff version, router allocation, acceptable price/quantity, maximum mark age and sequence constraints, and explicit opt-in for a resting order to match during the paid early window. A paid taker cannot execute a public-only stale quote during an undisclosed price update. Separate eligibility views/books or enforce an equivalent explicit maker authorization. Public-only matching uses the public packet and auditable public scheduling; when risk guard sees unsafe divergence, pause/requote rather than permit adverse fills. All participants in a given tier have equal access terms. No customer-order preview or federation/operator proprietary use of confidential pending orders; data signers do not decide execution priority. Public liquidation/funding/maturity references remain on their own authenticated common schedule. Clear emergency pause/exit paths. Do not automatically stack a 200 ms order delay on the 200 ms feed lag: compare feed-only, scheduler-only and combined arms, explicitly disclose end-to-end service difference. HFT-01 hard-coded 0.2/2 bps and delay remain historical control; this file does not activate a new matching policy.

## LN fees and commercial model

Use HFT-03 exact 100 ms batch, with one new packet digest per fill (bind both execution-view tier and signed fee cap into future scoped intents). For actual fills, maker 20 ppm and taker 200 ppm are candidate paid service rates, standard zero. Integer sat fee and cumulative maker rounding remain HFT-01; no fee on rejection/cancel. For every fill, gross charge F = F_m + F_t. Oracle share floor(0.70 F), router receives remainder. Exact conservation. One service charge, not a duplicate feed charge hidden in DLC settlement. Other competing routers may advertise independently signed alternative routing tariffs and a feed subscription/minimum under explicit terms; a fee from the same trade cannot be silently charged twice. No mandatory tax on all DLC settlement or unilateral transfer of trader collateral. The router and federation agree a signed meter and fee policy; subscribers can alternatively purchase a distinct feed subscription with clear billing. Providers can charge subscription based on service, subject to rights.

Use canonical registered payout descriptors to divide federation pool among actual infrastructure operators and a shared maintenance/data budget, with public receipts and auditable costs. No per-price outcome incentives or compensation for publishing a particular direction. Example launch budget allocation only: 50% of federation proceeds retained for provider licensing, shared oracle/prover/watchtower upkeep, 50% paid to service operators under agreed accounting. SHZ/TradeLayer earns its disclosed share for operating nodes, a reference router or feed subscription service; no assumed exclusive founder entitlement. A signed 100 ms batch commits exact DLC legs, trader margins, fee obligations, oracle packet IDs and recipient policy version. Accrue fees as provisional liabilities until independently authenticated LN commitment confirmation. Separate recognized revenue from accrued, failed/replayed commitments and refunds. Aggregate small obligations into economical LN updates; no payment per packet or per individual order. Capacity/credit/HTLC limits, retries, crash-safe journal and atomic state transition remain prerequisites. Keep user principal, contract payouts and fees separately accounted. Nova/SPV checks audit the packet/fee transcript after the fact; existing hourly INDEX-03 relation does not yet prove the high-rate stream or LN fee collection. New domain-separated packet roots and proof relation are required.

For daily matched notional V and paid maker/taker notional fractions m,t, before rounding, costs and collection failures:
gross = V*(0.00002*m+0.0002*t);
federation = 0.7*gross;
router pool = 0.3*gross.
At $10m/day, m=t=20%, gross $440/day, federation $308/day ($9240/30d), router pool $132/day. The founder's actual revenue is contractually assigned compensation net of operating costs, not the gross federation pool. Test adoption by market regime, not fixed time percentage: 0.1% shock time or 2% volatile time can carry disproportionate matched notional. Candidate mid-tier 50 ppm taker for a separately registered experiment, with fee/latency curve estimated from timestamped L2 market data and no hindsight/availability leakage. No claim of demand or profitability from synthetic volume.

## Deployment and compliance

Independent router operators, non-custodial channels and geographic proximity reduce dependence on one service and permit local latency competition. Derivatives market regulation, market data licensing, market conduct, sanctions and applicable licensing/registration obligations remain jurisdiction- and activity-dependent; separate roles or protocol decentralization do not grant exemptions. Before launch get qualified counsel on the actual trading, data and fee arrangements; disclose paid priority, prevent manipulation, maintain fair-dealing/record retention controls. Use Testnet4 and synthetic inputs until operational/security/legal gates pass.
