## Fixed-cadence publication

For each book side, filter resting quotes with at least $100 remaining, unexpired and inside the current fresh authenticated relay collar. Require two distinct maker public keys, then use the second-best qualifying price by native book priority. Round a bid down and ask up to a $50 grid. Suppress if fewer than two eligible keys. No IDs, counts, precise sizes or exact prices in the public payload. Presence arm publishes only two availability bits, discarding price fields. Band arm publishes two optional band values with at most 42 logical payload bits (21 per side, including missing; max price $100m). Timing/headers, transport authentication, frequency enforcement, correlated observations, identity splitting, collusion and market inference remain separate threat models. Distinct keys do not establish distinct humans or formal privacy. Public method is local research publisher; never expose as a client-driven arbitrary size/price RPC. Authentic relay signatures do not authenticate the hint itself. Production requires signed scoped publisher snapshots, monotonic epochs and anti-equivocation.

Sample after mark update before arrivals and due events, every 50/200/500 ms. Deliver at sample time + 0/100 ms; discard hints older than 250 ms. Presence policy picks among valid advertised opposite sides via deterministic independent tie-break. Band policy ranks by conservative price bound meeting the taker's signed limit, then same tie-break. Fall back to blind assignment when no admissible hint. No future data or access to private book state in the chooser. A stale hint can yield no fill; no reservation implied. All orders still observe 200 ms differentiated delay. Maker/taker identities disjoint in synthetic trace; self-match and collateral checked at execution.
