## Exact arithmetic

The optimization preserves upstream Montgomery residues mod P=15*2^27+1, original round constants, 24-cell width, 16-cell rate, 8-cell output, 8 full and 21 partial rounds, x^7 S-box and upstream Fiat-Shamir RNG. Montgomery multiplication computes the standard reduced product with 64-bit intermediate t+red*P < 2^64. Each external 4x4 block's un-reduced output has coefficient sum at most 16; final block-plus-column sum is at most 112*(P-1)<2^38. Thus performing additions and small integer scaling in u64 and reducing once per output is algebraically identical to all intermediate modular reductions. For the internal layer, sum all 24 cells in u64 and reduce once, then apply the original diagonal Montgomery multiplication. This changes implementation cost only. Empty and partial sponge blocks match the upstream overwrite-mode sponge. Reject noncanonical digest words, never reduce malformed proof words into valid input. No cryptographic parameters change.
