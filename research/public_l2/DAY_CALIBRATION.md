# Two-day public BTC L2 calibration

Follow-up to PR #28. Fixed full UTC dates October 11 and November 23, 2026, selected before downloading the 48 hours. They are exploratory dates, not a representative random sample or verified event timestamps. Four parallel runners, 48 one-hour downloads, 200MB per-file ceiling. Failed hours are retained as missing in the 86,400-second denominators. Require clean native continuity; never substitute or tune missing hours. Only aggregate reports and hashes retained.

Uses public-source v1 pilot with two corrections: explicit one-second right-edge missing time per hour, and fee-adjusted 100-vs-200ms saving uses incremental 1.5bp (rather than total 2bp). The latter does not affect prior tier winners or 100/300 results. Reconstruct each hour independently; 30-second causal feature warmup at each hour, counted missing. No price series or private data published.

One-second direction remains prior valid mid movement. For $1k target, score = max(abs(r1s), abs(r5s)/sqrt(5), abs(r30s)/sqrt(30)), all observed strictly before decision. Fixed score bins 0.1/0.25/0.5/1/2/5/10/20bp. Aggregate common-full-fill fee-adjusted savings and ex-post winners per bin. Train a simple causal fee choice on Oct 11 only: select maximum mean net savings vs free, with at least 100 common fills and >=95% common fill fraction of that bin; otherwise free. Freeze bin decisions and apply to Nov 23. Report held-out mean saving conditional on common fills, counts, excluded data and ex-post winners. No live profit or actual queue inference. Full-day denominators include failed hours and right edges; conditional results cannot substitute for a representative multi-week study. This public pilot is independent of any private study.
