# Public L2 latency calibration pilot

Independent public-source research, with no imported private archives or strategy data. Four fixed exploratory BTC hours: provider's published sample 2026-09-02 12 UTC, 2026-10-11 12 UTC, and 2026-11-23 12/20 UTC. The last two are calendar samples, not identified news-event windows. No claim of representativeness or historical study completion.

Public source: https://www.cryptohftdata.com/datasets/lighter-orderbook-data . The free REST endpoint supplies one compressed Parquet hour. Respect archive license; do not upload or commit source rows or reconstructible price sequences. CI retains only aggregate statistics and input hashes, deletes raw data. One file per job and 200MB cap, no keys, no private account access.

Sequence reconstruction requires native final and previous IDs and market symbol. Compare publication clock and conservative receive clock max(previous effective clock, exchange event ms, ceil(received ns/1e6), hour boundary). Strict-before events, no crossed/stale/unseeded books. At one-second times, both sides and causal sign of previous valid one-second mid move, fixed $100/$1k/$10k lot-rounded size and +/-20bp limit. Sweep at 100/140/200/300/600ms independently without consuming history. Report common-fill gross savings tails, fast-only/slow-only/neither/unknown counts, and retrospective full-fill tier winners over all clock-grid ticks. Tier schedules: SHZ candidate 0/.5/2bp at 300/200/100ms; venue comparison 0/.5/1.96bp at 300/300/140ms. No rate-limit effect modeled. Full displayed aggregate depth is not individual FOK availability, queue priority, market impact, actual fill or strategy P&L. Four hours cannot estimate a stable 0.1% tail; do not infer a calibrated distribution. Treat coverage gaps and endpoint failures transparently.

Selftest: python research/public_l2/pilot.py --selftest. Local use on one user-obtained BTC hourly file: python research/public_l2/pilot.py --book BTC_orderbook.parquet --hour 2026-09-02T12:00:00 --output summary.json. The research gate is exploratory; CI's fixture and archive jobs are distinct from any private historical replay.
