# SHZ-CG-01: capital cuts, route observability and settlement clocks

Research supplement for [Where the Capital Bound Moves](https://github.com/patrickdugan/Spiral/pull/21). Source base: [719b960](https://github.com/patrickdugan/SatsHedgeZero/tree/719b96088a8c91bde46b6de2681c7318fbc6982e). This is a read-only reference oracle; it does not change routing, signing, margin or consensus.

## 1. Model the actual backend

- `crates/shz-utxoref/src/pnl_batch.rs` constructs a source/vault/destination/sink graph. For each vault, budget C_v is the sum of its input UTXOs minus its allocated fee. Target demand is either protected sats plus all post-batch account equities at that vault, or an authorized external payout. A self-retention route is present with capacity C_v; cross-vault and payout routes are explicit and domain-checked. Preserve parallel routes as distinct edges; aggregate only when computing a cut. There is no transshipment through a destination in this graph. Surplus stays at its original vault.
- `crates/shz-utxoref/src/n_party_packer.rs` additionally requires spend authority, pinned outputs paired with their input indices, fees, and the CPFP anchor. A valid accounting flow does not by itself establish a valid transaction; a route graph for actual execution must be compiled from admissible signed objects.
- `crates/shz-dex/src/routing/clearing.rs` is a separate V3 venue ledger. Its cash, fund, claims, carry and signed open book satisfy cash - claims - fund + carry = book. Its default waterfall changes claim amounts and its deferred-claim payments depend on participating lanes. Never interpret a haircut as matched-service capital saving or treat cash, fund and claims as distinct physical collateral stocks. Its simulated adapter is not evidence of L1 recovery.
- The durable reservation book and active-chain confirmation determine release eligibility. A reserved or signed UTXO cannot be assumed reusable; freeze on ambiguity/reorg and reconcile the exact reserved snapshot, confirmed transaction and active-chain outpoints. In MNR-03 the offline signer's pinned output is conditional on the oracle and maturity, not arbitrary rerouting.

## 2. Exact cut certificate

Let J be any subset of destination vertices (including retained-vault liabilities); U_v(J) = sum of u_vj over edges into J. Define delta(J)=sum_{j in J} d_j - sum_v min(C_v,U_v(J)). The exact undelivered demand Delta is max_J delta(J), including J empty. Proof: in a cut with J on the sink side, every vault independently contributes C_v (if sink-side) or U_v(J) (if source-side), plus destination demand outside J. Minimize over vault side, then J, and apply max-flow/min-cut. Integral capacities give integral allocations. A feasible primal allocation of total D-Delta and any J attaining Delta are an independently checkable certificate: no need to rerun the solver to verify the pair. This certifies accounting feasibility for the supplied graph, never graph authenticity or spend authority.

For a one-sat increase in budget C_v, holding routes and demands fixed, Delta falls by one iff *every* maximizing J has U_v(J)>C_v. Each cut value falls by zero or one; a nonmaximal cut is at least one below the maximum. A saturated source edge or one particular min cut is insufficient to establish positive marginal value. For constant-cost unlimited substitute liquidity k per sat and free native routes, the one-sat saving is exactly k or zero. With heterogeneous costs or capacity-limited substitutes, solve the actual min-cost flow and report the finite perturbation, not an inferred market price.

For an independent rectangular uncertainty set C in [C-,C+], u in [u-,u+], d in [d-,d+], the worst deficit equals Delta(C-,u-,d+) by monotonicity and corner attainment. This assumes the adverse corner is admissible and permits allocation after observing the scenario. For coupled scenarios evaluate each admissible joint state; for precommitted routes require a separate common-allocation feasibility test. Never turn unauthenticated or stale capacity into positive lower bounds.

Example: C=(100,100), d=(100,100), both vaults can reach only target 0. Aggregate budgets cover total demand but J={1} certifies Delta=100. Injecting more funds into either vault has zero value until the missing route is restored.

## 3. Exact route reconstruction

Transfer the incidence/cycle-space argument of [EvalMath RHT-01/T1--T3](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/EvalMath/blob/958b3fc797cdc8fda927eff153d056b5e65be861/paper/reward_hacking_cohomology.md). Hold both observed used-vault and received-target totals fixed (if only budgets are known, include unused-stock variables or widen the model). Two feasible allocations differ by an integer circulation in the signed bipartite incidence matrix. With m positive-capacity edges, n vertices and c connected components, the unconstrained kernel has dimension m-n+c. Bounds can collapse a particular fibre; that dimension is not a claim that all flows are ambiguous. Revealing the nonforest edge amounts suffices to reconstruct all remaining amounts from the endpoint totals by leaf elimination.

For a route e=(v,j) with allocation x_e, remove e and all audited edges from the residual graph; for every other route f insert forward slack u_f-x_f and backward capacity x_f. Then its maximum feasible increase is min(u_e-x_e, maxflow(j,v)); its maximum decrease is min(x_e,maxflow(v,j)). Proof: changing e needs the opposite residual circulation path; decompose any feasible difference into signed residual arcs, and conversely append e to the returned flow. Thus exact coordinate ranges follow from two max flows per route, even with capacities, parallel edges and zero slack. Each range is separately attainable; endpoints of different ranges need not be jointly attainable.

On a 2x2 all-to-all unit graph, allocations (1,0,0,1) and (0,1,1,0) have identical endpoint totals. If only the diagonal has cost, the observed totals hide a two-unit cost difference. A single independent edge audit determines the entire allocation in this case. A cost linear in routes is identified by margins for every feasible box fibre iff its periods on all support cycles vanish (equivalently a vertex-potential cochain). Route authenticity still needs authenticated independent receipts.

## 4. Capital-time and admission

For each admissible scenario, expand vaults over event time and attach carry arcs only to capital that remains available under the same ownership and authorization. Lock/reservation, maturity, oracle, dispute, timeout, confirmation and reorg gates decide when transitions exist. Model atomic multi-input settlements as jointly gated actions; an ordinary flow relaxation that splits them gives only an optimistic feasibility bound. Count each physical satoshi once per time interval across trader, LP, fee/anchor and bond ownership; separately log claims. Integrate the resulting distinct locked stock in BTC-time and use matched delivered demand and loss/exit rights in the paper's fee bound.

Admission design: canonical graph and snapshot hash; checked satoshi conversions; independent primal/cut validation; complete replay-state binding; authorization and transaction verification; active-chain confirmation and reusability event. Certificates for shortfall are diagnostic, not permission to partially pay mandatory protected balances. No auto-execution or spending from this module.

## 5. Executable reference and evidence

`node research/capital_graph/check.mjs` is dependency-free. `graph.mjs` uses BigInt, validates amounts and returns delivered, deficit, a maximizing cut, per-route allocations, used vault totals and received target totals. `routeIntervals` takes exact fixed endpoint margins implicitly from a supplied feasible allocation and optionally pins audited route indices. It returns each route's exact coordinate range. This is not a Rust integration or a production optimizer.

The committed receipt records 6,561 complete 2x2 allocation instances (all budgets, demands and edge capacities in {0,1,2}), 13,122 one-sat perturbations, 1,296 coordinate-range checks against exhaustive fibres, and parallel-edge and exact-integer checks. The same JavaScript function bodies were evaluated before commit in an isolated JS runtime; repository Node CI and Rust/Core were not run. No new on-chain or testnet measurements.
